Connecting E-Receipt to your point-of-sale system eliminates the manual steps between a completed transaction and a delivered receipt. Once the integration is active, every checkout automatically triggers a branded digital receipt to your customer's email or phone — no staff action required, no separate receipt printer needed, and no missed deliveries.

## Square integration

The Square integration connects your Square account to E-Receipt via OAuth and maps each Square location to a merchant location in E-Receipt. Once authorized, every completed Square payment automatically creates and delivers a receipt.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations page">
    In the E-Receipt dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Square">
    Click the **Square** tile, then click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Square">
    You are redirected to Square's OAuth authorization screen. Sign in to your Square account and grant E-Receipt the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your locations">
    After authorization, E-Receipt lists all locations associated with your Square account. Toggle on each location you want to activate, and confirm the mapping to the corresponding E-Receipt merchant location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save**. E-Receipt begins listening for new transactions on all enabled locations immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What happens at checkout**

Every time a Square transaction is completed on an enabled location, E-Receipt automatically:

1. Retrieves the transaction details from the Square API.
2. Creates a receipt using your configured template.
3. Delivers the receipt to the customer via your chosen channel.

**Configuration options**

After connecting, you can adjust the following settings per location from **Settings → Integrations → Square → Configure**:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Delivery channel | Send receipts by email, SMS, or both |
| Receipt template | Select which branded template to use for Square transactions |
| Custom note | Add a location-specific message to every receipt (e.g., return policy, WiFi password) |

## Shopify POS integration

The Shopify POS integration is available as a Shopify app and is configured entirely within your Shopify admin — no manual API keys required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the E-Receipt app">
    Open the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com) and search for **E-Receipt**. Click **Add app** and follow the installation prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your E-Receipt account">
    After installation, open the E-Receipt app from your Shopify admin sidebar. Enter your E-Receipt API key when prompted, or click **Sign in with E-Receipt** to authenticate via OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure receipt settings">
    In the app settings, choose your default delivery channel, select a receipt template, and set any custom notes for in-store purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map customer profiles">
    Enable **Customer profile sync** to map each Shopify customer's email address to their E-Receipt customer profile. This ensures receipt history is consolidated across online and in-store purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable Shopify's native POS receipt">
    In **Shopify admin → Settings → Notifications**, disable the default POS receipt notification to prevent customers from receiving duplicate receipts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lightspeed integration

Lightspeed uses an API key-based connection rather than OAuth. Retrieve your key from the Lightspeed dashboard and paste it into E-Receipt to activate the integration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate an API key in Lightspeed">
    In your Lightspeed back office, navigate to **Configuration → API Access** and create a new API key with read access to transactions and customer data. Copy the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the key to E-Receipt">
    In the E-Receipt dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → Lightspeed** and paste your API key into the **Lightspeed API Key** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your outlet">
    E-Receipt fetches your Lightspeed outlets automatically. Enable each outlet you want to activate and map it to the corresponding E-Receipt merchant location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Click **Save**. E-Receipt confirms the connection is active and displays the timestamp of the last successful sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Testing your POS integration

Before going live, verify that the integration is working correctly using E-Receipt's test mode.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable test mode">
    In **Settings → Integrations**, toggle **Test mode** on for the POS integration you want to test. Test mode intercepts real transaction events but sends receipts only to the test email address or phone number you specify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process a sample transaction">
    Complete a test transaction in your POS system — use a $0.01 or voided transaction if your POS supports it, or simply complete a normal transaction while test mode is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm receipt creation">
    Open the E-Receipt dashboard and navigate to **Receipts**. You should see the receipt created from your test transaction, with delivery status showing the test destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable test mode">
    Once you have confirmed everything looks correct, toggle **Test mode** off. Subsequent transactions will deliver receipts to real customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Multi-location businesses:** each POS location (or outlet) maps to a separate merchant location in E-Receipt. This means you can assign a different receipt template, custom note, or delivery channel to each physical location. Manage location settings individually from **Settings → Locations**.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Disable your POS system's built-in receipt before going live.** Most POS systems send their own email or SMS receipt by default. If you leave the native receipt enabled alongside E-Receipt, your customers will receive two receipts for every transaction. Check your POS notification settings and turn off the default receipt as part of your activation checklist.
</Warning>
