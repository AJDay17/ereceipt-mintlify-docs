## Fraud reduction

Itemized digital receipts help both sides spot problems fast:

- **Customers** see exactly what they were charged for and can flag mystery entries before disputing with their bank.
- **Merchants** see fewer "I don't recognize this charge" chargebacks because customers can self-serve the answer from their app.

## Customer satisfaction

Removing the "What's your email?" prompt at the register speeds up checkout and eliminates a common source of friction. Customers who use eReceipt also get an ongoing record of every visit, which turns your business into a repeat destination they can revisit in the app.

## Environmental impact

Every eReceipt captured is one fewer thermal paper receipt printed. Thermal paper is not recyclable and often contains BPA — removing it from your checkout is a small operational change with a measurable environmental win.

<Note>
  Merchants keep their existing terminals and POS. eReceipt does not replace your payment stack — it enriches the customer-facing side of it.
</Note>
