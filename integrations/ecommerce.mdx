Merchants can create coupons in the eReceipt merchant tools for free. Coupons are then surfaced to customers automatically in the app based on location, favorites, and personal shopping history — no separate ad budget required.

## Where coupons appear

- **Nearby feed** — Customers browsing coupons around them see your active offers if they are physically near one of your locations.
- **Favorites feed** — Customers who have favorited your business see your offers pinned to their favorites tab.
- **Affiliate feed** — Larger brand-wide campaigns may also appear in the affiliate feed.

## Why publishing a coupon is worth it

Because eReceipt already knows which customers have shopped with you (via their linked-card receipts), coupons can be timed and targeted around real purchase behavior — not just impressions.

Typical use cases:

- **Bring back lapsed customers** — Offer a discount to customers who have not returned in the last 60 days.
- **Boost off-peak visits** — Publish a coupon that is only active during slow hours.
- **Reward loyal customers** — Highlight a "thank you" deal for repeat visitors.

## Creating a coupon

Coupon creation happens inside the merchant tools. The exact fields available may evolve, but generally include:

- Coupon title and description.
- Discount value or offer terms.
- Start and end dates.
- Optional location restrictions.

Once published, the coupon is automatically indexed into the customer-facing feeds. There is no separate distribution step.

<Note>
  Coupons in eReceipt are free to create — there is no per-impression or per-click charge for a merchant to publish.
</Note>
