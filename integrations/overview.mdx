## How eReceipt fits your checkout

eReceipt matches transactions to your business on the eReceipt side, using the customer's linked payment card and your merchant identifier. There is:

- **No new hardware** — you keep your existing terminals, POS, and payment processor.
- **No cashier training** — the checkout script does not change; cashiers do not ask for email or phone.
- **No app on the terminal** — nothing to install, update, or reboot.
