## How it works

Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to sending your first digital receipt.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.e-receipt.com](https://app.e-receipt.com) to create your E-Receipt account. No credit card is required to get started with the free tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your branding">
    Upload your logo, set your brand colors, and configure your receipt footer in the **Settings > Branding** section of the dashboard. Every receipt you send will reflect your business identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your store or use the API">
    Install one of our native integrations for platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, or Square — or use the E-Receipt REST API to trigger receipts directly from your own application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start sending receipts">
    Once your store or API connection is active, E-Receipt automatically generates and delivers a branded digital receipt to your customer the moment a transaction is recorded.
  </Step>
</Steps>
