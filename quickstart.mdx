eReceipt has two audiences with two very different starting points. Pick the guide that matches you.

## For customers

Get every receipt from every participating merchant into one app in under two minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Install eReceipt on your phone from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6587549323) or Google Play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Open the app and sign up with your email. Verify your email address to activate the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a payment card">
    In the app, tap **Link a card** and connect the credit or debit card you use most often. Card linking is powered by Plaid, so your card credentials are never stored by eReceipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay as usual">
    Next time you tap, swipe, or insert your linked card at a participating merchant, the itemized receipt appears in your eReceipt app automatically — no email prompt, no phone number, no paper slip needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  While you're in the app, browse the **Coupons** tab to discover deals from merchants near you, and try the **Expense Reporting** tools to tag and export receipts for reimbursements.
</Tip>

## For merchants

Offer paperless checkout and unlock customer analytics and coupon tools.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact sales">
    Reach out through the [merchant contact form](https://myereceipt.com/merchant/contact). Our team will confirm integration options for your payment processor and POS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your checkout">
    Work with our team to link your merchant identifier so card-linked transactions can be matched to your locations. Setup does not require a hardware change or a new app on your terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get access to the merchant tools">
    Once you're live, you can create free coupons, publish deals to nearby customers, and view analytics on customer retention, revenue trends, and lifetime value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Merchant onboarding is currently handled through our team. If you need a specific processor or POS supported, mention it in the contact form.
</Note>
