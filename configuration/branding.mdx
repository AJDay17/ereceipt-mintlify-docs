Strong branding builds customer trust and recognition — a receipt that looks and feels like your business reinforces professionalism and keeps your brand top of mind long after a purchase. E-Receipt gives you full control over your logo, color palette, typography, and footer content so every receipt is a seamless extension of your brand identity.

## Uploading your logo

Your logo appears prominently at the top of every receipt, in both email and SMS link previews.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Branding settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Branding > Logo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo file">
    Click **Upload Logo** and select your image file. E-Receipt accepts the following formats:

    | Format | Notes |
    |--------|-------|
    | **PNG** | Recommended for logos with transparency |
    | **SVG** | Best for scalability at any size |
    | **JPG** | Supported, but transparent backgrounds are not preserved |

    For best results, upload a file at **400 × 200 px** with a transparent background so the logo adapts cleanly to any background color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a dark-mode variant (optional but recommended)">
    Many email clients render in dark mode by default. Upload a second logo variant optimized for dark backgrounds under **Logo (Dark Mode)**. E-Receipt automatically serves the correct version based on the recipient's email client settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Logo**. The new logo applies to all receipts issued after this point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your primary logo is dark-colored, create a white or light variant for the dark-mode logo. This ensures your branding remains legible regardless of how a customer's email client renders the receipt.
</Tip>

## Brand colors

Your brand colors appear throughout the receipt email — on call-to-action buttons, divider lines, section headings, and accents. Enter your exact brand hex codes to maintain consistency with your other customer-facing materials.

Navigate to **Settings > Branding > Colors** and configure:

- **Primary color** — used for buttons (such as "View Receipt" or "Download PDF") and decorative accents. Enter a hex code (for example, `#1A73E8`) or click the color swatch to open the color picker.
- **Secondary color** — used for section headings within the receipt body (for example, "Items Purchased" or "Payment Summary"). Choose a color that complements your primary but provides enough contrast for readable text.

Click **Save Colors** when you are done. Changes appear in the live preview panel on the right side of the screen.

## Typography

Consistent typography reinforces brand personality. E-Receipt offers a curated selection of system-safe font families to ensure receipts render correctly across every email client, operating system, and device without requiring font downloads.

Navigate to **Settings > Branding > Typography** and choose:

- **Font family** — select from the available options (for example, Inter, Georgia, or system default). System fonts are strongly recommended for the broadest compatibility across email clients.
- **Font size** — choose from three presets:
  - **Small** — compact layout, well suited to dense itemized receipts.
  - **Medium** — the default; balances readability and information density.
  - **Large** — ideal for customers who prefer larger text, or for tablet-first display receipts.

## Footer customization

The receipt footer is valuable real estate for communicating return policies, linking to your social channels, and meeting email compliance requirements.

Navigate to **Settings > Branding > Footer** to configure:

- **Custom footer text** — add a short snippet of text such as a returns policy summary, a thank-you message, or a promotional note. For example: *"Returns accepted within 30 days with proof of purchase."*
- **Social media links** — add URLs for any social profiles you want to link (for example, Instagram, Facebook, or X). E-Receipt renders these as recognizable icons in the footer.
- **Unsubscribe link** — an unsubscribe link is **automatically included** in every receipt email to comply with CAN-SPAM, CASL, and similar regulations. You cannot remove it, but you can customize the surrounding text.

## Previewing your branding

Always review how your branding looks before sending receipts to customers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the preview">
    Navigate to **Settings > Branding > Preview**. A live rendered receipt updates in real time as you adjust settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test receipt">
    Click **Send Test Receipt** and enter your own email address. E-Receipt sends a sample receipt using your current branding so you can see exactly how it appears in a real inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check both light and dark mode">
    Open the test receipt in your email client and, if supported, toggle dark mode to verify both logo variants and your color choices render correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Test your receipt in multiple email clients — Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook render HTML differently. Services like Litmus or Email on Acid let you preview across dozens of clients simultaneously and catch rendering issues before they reach your customers.
</Tip>
