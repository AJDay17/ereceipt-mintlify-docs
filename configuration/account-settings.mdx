Your account settings control how E-Receipt behaves across your entire business — from the name and address printed on every receipt footer, to the API keys that authenticate your integrations, to the team members who manage your account day to day. Take a few minutes to configure these settings before you start issuing receipts so everything is accurate from your very first transaction.

## Business profile

Your business profile supplies the identity information that appears on every receipt you send. Keep this information accurate and up to date so customers always know who the receipt is from.

Navigate to **Settings > Business Profile** and fill in the following fields:

- **Business name** — your trading name as it should appear on receipts.
- **Address** — your registered or primary business address, used in the receipt footer.
- **Website** — your public-facing URL, rendered as a clickable link in email receipts.
- **Support email** — the address customers should contact for receipt queries. This appears in the receipt footer and as the reply-to address on outbound emails.

Save your changes and they will apply to all receipts issued from that point forward.

## Timezone and currency

E-Receipt stamps every receipt with the date and time of the transaction. Setting the correct timezone ensures those timestamps match your local business hours rather than UTC.

Navigate to **Settings > Regional** to configure:

- **Default timezone** — choose the timezone that matches your primary place of business (for example, `America/New_York` or `Europe/London`). If you operate multiple locations in different timezones, you can override this at the individual merchant-location level.
- **Default currency** — enter the [ISO 4217](https://www.iso.org/iso-4217-currency-codes.html) currency code for your primary trading currency (for example, `USD`, `EUR`, or `GBP`). Currency codes are displayed alongside amounts on every receipt.

<Note>
  You can pass a `currency` field per receipt via the API to override the account default. This is useful if you accept payments in multiple currencies.
</Note>

## API keys

API keys authenticate requests your systems make to the E-Receipt API. You should create a separate key for each integration or environment so you can rotate or revoke individual keys without disrupting everything else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open API Keys settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > API Keys** in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new key">
    Click **New API Key** and give it a descriptive name that identifies its purpose — for example, `Production`, `POS Integration`, or `Staging`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the key immediately">
    Your new API key is displayed **only once**. Copy it and store it securely in your secrets manager or environment configuration before closing the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the key in your requests">
    Pass the key in the `Authorization` header of every API request:

    ```http
    Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can **rotate** a key (generate a replacement and invalidate the old one) or **revoke** a key entirely at any time from the API Keys settings page.

<Warning>
  Never share your API keys publicly or commit them to source control. If a key is exposed, revoke it immediately from **Settings > API Keys** and generate a replacement.
</Warning>

## Team members

You can invite colleagues to access your E-Receipt account and assign them a role that matches their responsibilities.

### Inviting a team member

Navigate to **Settings > Team** and click **Invite Member**. Enter the person's email address and select a role, then click **Send Invite**. They will receive an email prompting them to accept the invitation and set up their login.

### Roles and permissions

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access to all settings, billing, API keys, and team management. Only one Owner per account. |
| **Admin** | Can manage branding, templates, integrations, team members, and API keys. Cannot change billing or transfer ownership. |
| **Member** | Can view the dashboard and receipt history, and issue receipts via the dashboard. Cannot access settings, API keys, or billing. |

### Changing roles and removing members

To change a team member's role, navigate to **Settings > Team**, click the member's name, select a new role from the dropdown, and save. To remove a member, click **Remove** next to their name. Removed members lose access immediately.

## Billing

Your billing settings give you visibility into your current plan, usage, and payment history.

Navigate to **Settings > Billing** to:

- **View your current plan** — see which tier you are on and your usage for the current billing period (receipts sent, SMS messages, active team members).
- **Upgrade or downgrade** — click **Change Plan** to move to a higher or lower tier. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
- **Cancel your subscription** — click **Cancel Subscription** to stop recurring charges. Your account remains active until the end of the paid period.
- **Download invoices** — all past invoices are listed in the **Invoice History** table. Click **Download PDF** next to any invoice to save a copy for your records.

<Note>
  E-Receipt provides a **sandbox environment** for testing that is completely separate from your production account. Receipts sent in the sandbox are never delivered to real customers and do not count toward your plan usage. Switch between environments using the toggle in the top navigation bar of your dashboard. Sandbox API keys begin with `sk_test_` and production keys begin with `sk_live_`.
</Note>
