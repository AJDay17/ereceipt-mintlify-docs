Merchants who offer eReceipt at checkout get access to analytics built on real, itemized transaction data. Because every receipt is linked to a specific customer, the analytics answer questions ordinary payment reports cannot — such as who is a returning customer, who has churned, and what any given cohort is worth over time.

## Why the data is different

Traditional payment reports show anonymous card swipes and totals. eReceipt merchant analytics show:

- Whether a transaction was a first visit or a return visit.
- How long ago the same customer was last in the store.
- Which items make up the transaction.
- How the customer's spending compares to their historical average.

That richer signal is what makes retention, lifetime value, and cohort-based reporting possible.

## Where to access analytics

Merchant analytics live in the merchant dashboard. Coverage and cadence of specific reports can evolve — the eReceipt team publishes changes in-product.

<Note>
  Analytics are only populated for transactions matched to eReceipt customers. Payments from customers who have not linked a card do not appear in these reports, but the aggregate metrics remain representative of your linked-card cohort.
</Note>
