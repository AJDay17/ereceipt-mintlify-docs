Coupons in eReceipt are more than just push notifications — they are surfaced contextually, so customers see the right offers in the right places. This page walks through how coupons are organized in the app.

## Nearby

The **Nearby** tab shows deals from participating merchants in the customer's current area. The list updates based on location, so opening the app in a new neighborhood surfaces the merchants around you.

## Affiliate

The **Affiliate** tab shows offers from partner brands. These are typically larger promotions or brand-wide campaigns, not tied to a specific local branch.

## Favorites

Any merchant a customer has favorited (by tapping the heart in the receipts feed or search) shows up in the **Favorites** tab. Customers see new deals from their regular spots without hunting for them.

## Referral

The **Referral** tab lets customers invite friends to eReceipt and earn rewards when those friends sign up and link a card. The specific referral reward is set by the platform.

## For merchants

Merchants can create and publish their own coupons for free from the merchant tools. See [Merchant coupons](/integrations/overview) for how coupons appear on the merchant side and how to launch a campaign.

<Note>
  Coupons and deals are curated in-app; there is no separate coupon marketplace to browse. New offers appear automatically for customers whose location or preferences match.
</Note>
