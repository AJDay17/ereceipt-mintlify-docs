E-Receipt delivers digital receipts to your customers through two channels: email and SMS. Email sends a fully branded HTML receipt directly to the customer's inbox, while SMS delivers a short message with a link to an online receipt viewer. You choose the channel at send time, or let the customer's saved delivery preference decide automatically. Both channels support immediate sending, scheduled delivery, and full delivery status tracking.

## Send a receipt immediately

Once a receipt is in draft status, you can send it with a single action from the dashboard or a single API call.

**From the dashboard:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the receipt">
    Navigate to **Receipts** and click on the receipt you want to deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Click the **Send** button in the top-right corner of the receipt detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a delivery channel">
    Select **Email** or **SMS** from the channel picker. If the customer has a preferred channel saved, it will be pre-selected. Click **Confirm Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Via the API:**

Send a `POST` request to the send endpoint, specifying the delivery channel in the request body.

```bash
POST https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/{id}/send
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
Content-Type: application/json
```

```json
{
  "channel": "email"
}
```

Or to send via SMS:

```json
{
  "channel": "sms"
}
```

**Example response:**

```json
{
  "receipt_id": "rcp_x7k2mn9p",
  "channel": "email",
  "delivery_status": "queued",
  "queued_at": "2025-03-15T10:25:00Z"
}
```

The `delivery_status` of `"queued"` confirms that the receipt has been accepted and handed off to the delivery pipeline. It will move through further status states as it progresses toward the customer.

## Delivery channels

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    E-Receipt sends email receipts as fully rendered **HTML emails** built from your active template. The email is compatible with all major email clients, including Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and mobile clients.

    **Subject line:** The default subject line is `"Your receipt from [Merchant Name]"`. You can customize it in **Settings > Email** or override it per send by passing `"subject"` in the send request body:

    ```json
    {
      "channel": "email",
      "subject": "Thanks for stopping by — here's your receipt"
    }
    ```

    **From name:** Set your preferred sender name in **Settings > Email**. This is the name customers see in their inbox (e.g., `"Maple Street Café"`). The reply-to address can also be configured to route customer replies to your support inbox.

    **Content:** The email body mirrors your receipt template exactly, including your logo, brand colors, line items table, totals, and footer notes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="SMS">
    E-Receipt sends SMS receipts as a short text message containing a secure, shortened link to an online receipt viewer. The full branded receipt renders in the customer's mobile browser when they tap the link.

    **Character limit:** The SMS message body is kept under 160 characters to avoid multi-part message splitting. The default message format is:

    ```
    Your receipt from [Merchant Name]: https://rcpt.io/r/abc123
    ```

    You can customize the message prefix in **Settings > SMS**.

    **Short link:** Every SMS receipt includes a unique short link that tracks whether the customer has viewed the receipt. Links expire after 12 months but the receipt remains accessible in the customer's profile.

    **Opt-in requirement:** You may only send SMS receipts to customers who have explicitly opted in to SMS communications from your business. E-Receipt enforces this automatically — if a customer's `sms_opted_in` field is `false`, the send request will return a `422` error rather than deliver the message.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Scheduling a send

To deliver a receipt at a future date and time, add the `send_at` field to your send request. The value must be an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp.

```bash
POST https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/{id}/send
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
Content-Type: application/json
```

```json
{
  "channel": "email",
  "send_at": "2025-03-15T14:00:00Z"
}
```

The response will include `"delivery_status": "scheduled"` and a `scheduled_for` timestamp confirming the queued send time. You can cancel a scheduled send by calling `DELETE https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/{id}/send` before the scheduled time.

## Delivery status tracking

E-Receipt tracks each receipt through the full delivery lifecycle. The `delivery_status` field on a receipt object reflects the most recent state.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| `queued` | The send request was accepted and the message is waiting to be dispatched. |
| `sent` | The message has been handed off to the email or SMS carrier. |
| `delivered` | The carrier confirmed successful delivery to the recipient's inbox or device. |
| `viewed` | The customer opened the email or clicked the SMS receipt link. |
| `failed` | Delivery could not be completed. Check the `failure_reason` field for details. |

To check the current delivery status of a receipt, call `GET https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/{id}` and read the `delivery_status` field:

```bash
GET https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/rcp_x7k2mn9p
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
```

```json
{
  "id": "rcp_x7k2mn9p",
  "delivery_status": "delivered",
  "sent_at": "2025-03-15T10:25:03Z",
  "delivered_at": "2025-03-15T10:25:09Z",
  "first_viewed_at": null
}
```

## Handling failures

When a delivery attempt fails, E-Receipt sets `delivery_status` to `"failed"` and populates the `failure_reason` field with a machine-readable code. Common failure reasons include:

- **`invalid_email`** — The email address on the customer profile is malformed or the domain does not exist.
- **`email_bounced`** — The receiving mail server rejected the message (hard bounce). This often indicates the address no longer exists.
- **`customer_unsubscribed`** — The customer clicked the unsubscribe link in a previous email and has opted out of all email communications.
- **`sms_opt_out`** — The customer replied STOP to a previous SMS, revoking their consent.
- **`invalid_phone`** — The phone number on the customer profile is not a valid mobile number for SMS delivery.

**Retrying a failed receipt:**

- **Dashboard:** Open the receipt, review the failure reason displayed in the delivery status banner, correct the customer's contact details if needed, and click **Resend**.
- **API:** Fix the customer's contact information via `PATCH https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/customers/{id}`, then call `POST https://api.e-receipt.com/v1/receipts/{id}/send` again with the desired channel.

<Warning>
  SMS delivery is subject to **opt-in compliance requirements** under regulations including TCPA (United States), CASL (Canada), and GDPR (European Union). You must obtain explicit, documented consent from customers before sending them SMS receipts. E-Receipt blocks SMS sends to any customer whose `sms_opted_in` flag is `false`, but it is your responsibility to collect and record consent through a compliant mechanism before setting that flag to `true`. Failure to comply with applicable regulations may result in significant legal penalties.
</Warning>
