Card linking is what turns eReceipt from an app that stores receipts into an app that captures them automatically. This page explains what happens during the linking flow, what data eReceipt sees, and what happens if a customer replaces or removes a card.

## Linking a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start card linking in the app">
    In the eReceipt app, go to **Settings → Linked cards** and tap **Link a card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your bank in Plaid">
    eReceipt hands off to **Plaid**, the same service used by apps like Venmo, Robinhood, and Chime. Search for your bank and sign in with your bank credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Confirm the account and card you want to link. Plaid returns a secure token to eReceipt. Your bank credentials are never seen or stored by eReceipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're done">
    From this moment on, transactions on the linked card at participating merchants generate receipts automatically in your eReceipt feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What eReceipt sees — and what it doesn't

| eReceipt sees | eReceipt does **not** see |
| --- | --- |
| A secure token that identifies the linked card | Your bank username or password |
| Transaction merchant, amount, and timestamp on the linked card | Your full card number |
| Which linked card was used for a given transaction | Your full account or routing number |

All bank authentication is handled by Plaid; eReceipt never receives credentials.

## Multiple cards

Customers can link more than one card. Every linked card independently produces receipts, and each receipt records which card was used (card type \+ last four digits).

## Removing or replacing a card

- **Remove a card** — Go to **Settings → Linked cards** and tap **Remove**. Historical receipts stay in your feed, but new transactions on that card will no longer be captured.
- **Card reissued or replaced** — If your bank issues a new card number (for example, after loss or fraud), re-link the new card. eReceipt will resume capturing receipts from the next transaction.

## OAuth return paths

During Plaid linking, some banks require an OAuth redirect that returns control to the eReceipt app. That redirect uses one of these paths depending on the app variant:

- Production app → `myereceipt.com/plaid-oauth/prod`
- Preview build → `myereceipt.com/plaid-oauth/preview`
- Development build → `myereceipt.com/plaid-oauth/dev`

You should never need to visit these URLs manually — the app opens them automatically.

<Note>
  If a Plaid OAuth redirect ever fails to reopen the app, the same URL renders a fallback page in your browser that finishes the linking flow.
</Note>
