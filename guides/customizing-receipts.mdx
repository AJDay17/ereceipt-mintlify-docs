Once receipts start flowing into your eReceipt app, the app is designed to make them useful — not just archived. This page covers the everyday actions customers take on receipts.

## Search and filter

The receipts feed supports search across merchant name, item names, and dates. You can also filter by:

- **Merchant** — see every receipt from a single business.
- **Date range** — narrow to a week, month, or custom range.
- **Amount** — find transactions above or below a threshold.
- **Card** — filter by which linked card was used.

## Favorites

Tap the heart on any merchant to save them as a favorite. Favorited merchants surface first in the **Coupons** tab, so you're more likely to see deals from places you already shop.

## Tag and export for expense reports

For business or reimbursable expenses, receipts can be:

- **Tagged** with a category (e.g., _Travel_, _Client meal_, _Office supplies_).
- **Bulk-selected** and exported as a PDF or CSV.
- **Shared** directly to email or a third-party app like your finance team's inbox.

This replaces the common workflow of taking photos of paper receipts and hunting them down at month end.

## Recognize charges

Every receipt shows the merchant, branch, and itemized purchases behind a card charge. If you ever see an unfamiliar entry on your card statement, cross-referencing it with the eReceipt feed usually resolves the mystery in seconds — and if it does not, the itemized data makes it much easier to dispute the charge with your bank.
