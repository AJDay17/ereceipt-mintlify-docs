eReceipt does not ask the customer or the merchant to _do_ anything extra at checkout. Instead, receipts are created automatically by matching real card transactions to the merchant's itemized sales data. This page explains what actually happens end-to-end.

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer links a card">
    A customer connects a credit or debit card to the eReceipt app via **Plaid**. Plaid brokers the connection so eReceipt only receives a secure token, never the card number or bank credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer pays at a participating merchant">
    The customer completes checkout as normal — tap, insert, or swipe. The cashier does not ask for an email or a phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transaction event is captured">
    Once the transaction settles on the linked card, eReceipt receives a transaction event that includes the merchant identifier, timestamp, and total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merchant sale is matched">
    eReceipt matches the transaction to the merchant's itemized sale record for that exact time and amount, at that exact branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt is delivered to the app">
    A fully itemized digital receipt is created and pushed into the customer's eReceipt app feed within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a receipt contains

Each receipt in the app includes:

- **Merchant** — company name, branch, logo, phone, and address.
- **Date and time** — timestamp of the transaction.
- **Line items** — each item's name, quantity, unit price, subtotal, and tax.
- **Totals** — subtotal, tax, and grand total.
- **Payment** — card type (Visa, Mastercard, etc.), last four digits, authorization code, and transaction ID.
- **External ID** — the merchant's POS transaction identifier, used for reconciliation.

## Why nothing has to change at the register

Because matching happens on the eReceipt side using the customer's linked-card token, cashiers do not need to know whether a customer uses eReceipt. There is no button to press, no app to install on the terminal, and no additional field to enter at checkout. Both sides of the transaction stay exactly as they were.

<Note>
  A receipt only appears if the customer has linked the specific card they are paying with. Payments on unlinked cards, cash, or store gift cards do not produce eReceipt receipts.
</Note>
