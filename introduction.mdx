E-Receipt is a cloud-based platform that enables retailers, restaurants, and e-commerce merchants to create, customize, and deliver digital receipts to their customers instantly — no paper, no printing, no hassle. Instead of handing over a paper slip that gets lost or thrown away, you send a beautifully branded receipt directly to your customer's email inbox or mobile phone. E-Receipt solves the twin problems of paper waste and poor post-purchase customer experience by making every receipt fast, trackable, and on-brand.

## How E-Receipt works

E-Receipt fits seamlessly into your existing transaction flow. When a transaction occurs — whether at a physical till, through an online checkout, or via an API call — E-Receipt instantly creates a structured receipt record. That record is populated with your merchant details, the line items from the sale, and any custom fields you've configured. E-Receipt then applies your chosen template to render a polished, branded receipt and delivers it to the customer's email address or phone number. The customer opens a clean, mobile-friendly receipt on any device, and you can see exactly when it was delivered and viewed — all in real time from the dashboard.

## Supported delivery methods

E-Receipt supports two primary delivery channels out of the box. You can enable one or both per customer, and customers can update their own preferences through the self-service receipt portal.

| Feature | Email | SMS |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery speed | Seconds | Seconds |
| Rich formatting (logo, colors, tables) | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (plain text link) |
| Receipt link included | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Customer reply support | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Delivery status tracking | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Open / view tracking | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (link click) |
| Cost | Included in all plans | Requires SMS add-on or qualifying plan |
