eReceipt is a card-linked digital receipt platform built by TradeNexus, Corp. Customers link a payment card to the eReceipt mobile app, and every time they tap or swipe that card at a participating merchant, a fully itemized digital receipt appears in the app automatically. There is no "What's your email?" prompt at the register, no phone number to type, and no paper slip to lose.

For customers, eReceipt is a place where every receipt, deal, and spending record lives in one app. For merchants, it is a way to deliver a modern checkout experience while unlocking customer analytics, targeted coupons, and reduced paper costs.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer pays">
    A customer taps, swipes, or inserts their linked card at checkout — exactly like they normally would.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt captured">
    eReceipt automatically links the transaction to the customer's card. No email or phone number is requested at the register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Instant access">
    The itemized digital receipt appears in the customer's eReceipt app within seconds of the transaction settling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    No paper waste, no lost receipts, and no manual data entry — on either side of the counter.
  </Step>
</Steps>
